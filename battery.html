📖 Batteries in Computing

In computing, batteries serve two major roles: the rechargeable pack in laptops and tablets that enables portability, and the UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) battery that protects desktop systems from power outages, protecting data and hardware from power surges.

💡 Did You Know?

Lithium-ion batteries degrade with charge cycles. Keeping a laptop battery between 20–80% charge can double its lifespan from ~500 cycles to ~1000 cycles.
