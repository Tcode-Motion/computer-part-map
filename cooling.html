Cooling Systems

Keeping your hardware chill under pressure — thermal management for peak performance.

📖 Why Is Cooling Important?

Computer components generate heat as they process electricity. If they get too hot, they suffer from thermal throttling (slowing down to save themselves) or even permanent damage. A good cooling system ensures your CPU and GPU can run at maximum speed for long periods.
