📖 What is a CPU?

The Central Processing Unit (CPU) is the primary component responsible for executing instructions in a computer program. It performs arithmetic, logic, control, and input/output operations as specified by the program. Modern CPUs can execute billions of operations per second and are built using transistors just a few nanometers in size.

Every action you take on a computer — opening a browser, playing a game, running AI models — is ultimately processed by the CPU. It fetches instructions from RAM, decodes them, executes them, and writes results back.

💡 Did You Know?

The Intel 4004, launched in 1971, was the world's first commercial microprocessor. It ran at 740 kHz and had just 2,300 transistors. Today's CPUs have over 50 billion transistors and run at 5+ GHz.
