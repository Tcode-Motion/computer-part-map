📖 What is a GPU?

A Graphics Processing Unit (GPU) is a specialized processor designed to handle parallel computations at massive scale. Originally built to render graphics, GPUs are now central to gaming, video editing, scientific simulation, machine learning, and cryptocurrency mining.

While a CPU has 8–24 powerful cores for sequential tasks, a GPU has thousands of smaller cores optimized for doing many things simultaneously — perfect for rendering millions of pixels or training neural networks.

💡 Did You Know?

NVIDIA's H100 GPU, used for AI training, contains 80 billion transistors and costs over $30,000. A single H100 can train a large language model 30x faster than the previous generation.
