HDD — Hard Disk Drive

The classic spinning disk — still the best value for mass storage and backups.

📖 What is an HDD?

A Hard Disk Drive (HDD) stores data on spinning magnetic platters. A read/write head moves across the platters to access data. HDDs are much slower than SSDs but offer enormous storage at very low cost — making them ideal for backups, NAS systems, and bulk media storage.

💡 Did You Know?

IBM introduced the first HDD in 1956 — it weighed over 900 kg, held 5 MB of data, and cost $10,000 per month to lease. Today, you can buy 20 TB for under ₹25,000.
