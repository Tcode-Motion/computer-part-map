🍎 macOS

Apple's UNIX-based desktop OS — from Mac OS X (2001) to macOS Sequoia (2024).

📖 What is macOS?

macOS is Apple's proprietary desktop operating system. Built on a UNIX base, it is renowned for its stability, security, and tight ecosystem integration.
