📖 What is a Microphone?

A microphone is an input device that converts sound (air pressure waves) into electrical signals. Used for voice calls, podcasting, streaming, recording music, voice assistants, and gaming communication. The two main transducer types are condenser (sensitive, studio-quality) and dynamic (rugged, handles loud volumes).

💡 Did You Know?

The cardioid polar pattern (most common in USB mics) picks up sound primarily from the front while rejecting noise from the sides and rear — perfect for home recording where you can't fully control the room's acoustics.
