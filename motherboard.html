📖 What is a Motherboard?

The motherboard (or mobo) is the main printed circuit board (PCB) in a computer. It holds and allows communication between many of the crucial electronic components of a system, such as the CPU and memory, and provides connectors for other peripherals.

💡 Did You Know?

The first motherboard is often considered to be the "Planar" board used in the IBM Personal Computer released in 1981. It held the CPU and RAM but had very few built-in ports compared to modern boards.
