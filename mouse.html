📖 What is a Mouse?

A computer mouse is a pointing device that detects motion and translates it into cursor movement on screen. Modern mice use optical or laser sensors to track movement with extreme precision — gaming mice can register up to 36,000 DPI and poll at 8,000 Hz (once every 0.125ms).

💡 Did You Know?

The first mouse was invented by Douglas Engelbart in 1964. It was a wooden block with two perpendicular wheels. He called it a "mouse" because its wire resembled a tail.
