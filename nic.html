🌐 Networking

NIC — Network Interface Card

Your computer's physical gateway to networks — Ethernet at 1G, 2.5G, or 10G.

📖 What is a NIC?

A Network Interface Card (NIC) is a hardware component that allows a computer to connect to a network — either via Ethernet (wired) or Wi-Fi (wireless). Every modern motherboard includes a built-in NIC, but dedicated add-in NICs offer higher speeds (2.5G, 10G) and better reliability for servers, NAS, and enthusiast builds.

💡 Did You Know?

A 10 Gigabit Ethernet (10GbE) NIC can transfer data at 1,250 MB/s — fast enough to completely fill a 1 TB SSD in under 15 minutes over a local network.
