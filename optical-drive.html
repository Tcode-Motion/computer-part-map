Optical Drive — CD/DVD/Blu-ray

Legacy but not dead — optical disc drives for backup, media, and legacy software.

📖 What is an Optical Drive?

An optical drive reads (and sometimes writes) data from optical discs — CDs, DVDs, and Blu-ray discs — using a laser. While largely replaced by USB drives and streaming for consumer use, optical drives remain important for legacy software, physical media collectors, and archival backup.

💡 Did You Know?

A single-layer Blu-ray disc holds 25 GB, while a 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray disc can hold 100 GB — enough for a full 4K movie with Dolby Atmos audio, something streaming often can't match in quality.
