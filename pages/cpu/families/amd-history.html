“‰ The Bulldozer Years (2011-2016)

AMD's Bulldozer architecture (2011) was a strategic disaster. It used a radical "module" design where two cores shared a single FPU and fetch unit, resulting in terrible IPC. AMD lost massive market share, and its stock dropped below $2.

CEO Lisa Su (appointed 2014) spearheaded the Zen project led by architect Jim Keller, which completely reversed AMD's fortunes. By 2023, AMD held ~30% desktop CPU market share — up from ~5% in 2016.
