ARM Architecture — From Phones to Supercomputers

ARM processors power 99% of smartphones, Apple Macs, AWS cloud servers, and the world's fastest supercomputer (Fugaku). Over 250 billion ARM chips have been shipped.

“– What is ARM?

ARM (Advanced RISC Machines) is a family of RISC instruction set architectures. Unlike Intel/AMD, ARM Holdings doesn't manufacture chips — they design the ISA and core blueprints, then license them to companies like Apple, Qualcomm, Samsung, and MediaTek who build their own chips.

””big.LITTLE & DynamIQ

ARM's big.LITTLE architecture (2011) pairs high-performance "big" cores with power-efficient "LITTLE" cores. The OS dynamically shifts workloads between them for optimal battery life. DynamIQ (2017) improved this by allowing flexible core configurations within a single cluster.
