3D Stacked CPUs — Building Upward

As lateral transistor scaling slows, the industry is going vertical — stacking dies on top of each other to increase density and bandwidth.

“– Why 3D Stacking?

Traditional 2D scaling (shrinking transistors) is reaching physical limits. 3D stacking adds a new dimension — building chips vertically rather than spreading out. This increases transistor density, shortens wire lengths (reducing latency), and enables heterogeneous integration (mixing memory and logic).
