Optical CPUs — Computing with Light

Optical (photonic) processors use photons instead of electrons to process data. They promise near-zero latency, ultra-low power, and speed-of-light computation.

“– Why Optical Computing?

Electronic transistors are hitting physical limits — heat, leakage current, and interconnect delays. Photonic computing uses light (photons) to carry and process information. Light travels faster, doesn't generate heat from resistance, and can carry multiple signals simultaneously using different wavelengths (colors).
