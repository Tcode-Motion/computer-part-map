CPU Clock Speed — GHz, Boost, and Turbo

Clock speed measures how many cycles a CPU completes per second. A 5 GHz CPU executes 5 billion cycles every second.

“– What is Clock Speed?

Clock speed (or clock rate) is the frequency at which a CPU's internal clock oscillates, measured in Hertz (Hz). Each tick of the clock signals the CPU to perform one step of an operation.

Modern CPUs operate in the GHz (Gigahertz) range — billions of cycles per second. Higher clock speed generally means faster single-threaded performance, but it's only one factor in overall speed (IPC and core count also matter).
