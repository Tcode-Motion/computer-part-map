CPU Overclocking — Beyond Factory Specs

Overclocking means running your CPU at higher frequencies than the manufacturer intended, extracting extra performance at the cost of heat and potentially stability.

“– What is Overclocking?

Overclocking increases the CPU's clock multiplier and/or base clock beyond factory settings. A CPU rated at 5.0 GHz might run stably at 5.5 GHz with adequate cooling and voltage adjustments.
