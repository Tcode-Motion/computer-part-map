CPU Registers — The Fastest Memory

Registers are tiny storage locations inside the CPU that hold data being actively processed. They operate at the speed of the clock — sub-nanosecond access.

“– What are CPU Registers?

Registers are the fastest memory in the entire computer hierarchy — faster than L1 cache, faster than RAM, faster than anything. They sit directly inside the CPU core and hold the data the ALU is currently working on.

A register access takes less than 1 clock cycle (~0.2 ns at 5 GHz). The CPU has a very limited number of registers — typically 16-32 architectural registers visible to the programmer, with hundreds more internal physical registers used by the CPU for out-of-order execution.
