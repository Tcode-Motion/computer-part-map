CPU Chip Design — Chiplets & Packaging

Modern CPUs are no longer single monolithic dies — they're made of multiple chiplets connected with advanced packaging technologies.

“– Monolithic vs Chiplet Design

Traditionally, CPUs were monolithic — everything on one die. As transistor counts grew, yields dropped (a single defect kills the whole chip). AMD pioneered the chiplet approach with Zen 2 (2019), splitting the CPU into smaller, independent dies connected via an I/O die.
