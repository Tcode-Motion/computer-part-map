CPU Fabrication — How Processors Are Made

From sand to silicon — the most complex manufacturing process on Earth, producing chips with billions of transistors at nanometer precision.

“– The Fabrication Process

CPU fabrication (or "fab") is the process of creating integrated circuits on silicon wafers. It takes place in clean rooms — facilities 10,000é cleaner than a hospital operating room. The entire process takes 3-4 months and involves hundreds of steps.

A single EUV lithography machine from ASML costs over $200 million, weighs 180 tons, and requires 3 Boeing 747s to ship. Only TSMC, Samsung, and Intel have them.

13Final Testing & BinningCPUs tested at various speeds; best become i9/Ryzen 9, others become i5/Ryzen 5
