Process Nodes — The Nanometer Race

The "nm" number represents the manufacturing process technology. Smaller nodes pack more transistors, use less power, and (usually) run faster.

“– What Does "nm" Mean?

Originally, the nanometer (nm) number referred to the actual gate length of a transistor. Today, it's more of a marketing name — TSMC's "5nm" doesn't actually have 5nm-wide features. The number indicates a generation of manufacturing technology with specific transistor density and performance characteristics.

’¡ Did You Know?

Intel's "Intel 7" process (used in 13th/14th Gen Core) is technically a 10nm process with density comparable to TSMC's 7nm. Intel renamed it to reduce confusion. A silicon atom is 0.2nm — transistors are only ~15-25 atoms wide at the 3nm node.
