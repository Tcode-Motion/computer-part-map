UPS — Uninterruptible Power Supply

Protects against power cuts, surges, and brownouts — essential for Indian power conditions.

“– What is a UPS?

A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) provides battery backup power when the main electrical supply fails. It gives you enough time to save work and safely shut down your system — preventing data loss, hardware damage, and file corruption from sudden power cuts.

In India, where power fluctuations and cuts are common, a UPS is almost mandatory for desktop PCs, networking equipment, and servers.

’¡ Did You Know?

A sudden power loss while Windows is writing to the disk can corrupt the entire file system, requiring a full reinstall. A UPS costing just ₹3,000 can prevent this catastrophic scenario.
