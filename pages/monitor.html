📖 What is a Monitor?

A monitor is the primary output device of a computer — it displays visual information by converting electrical signals into light. Modern monitors come in a variety of resolutions, panel types, refresh rates, and sizes, tailored for everything from competitive gaming to professional color work.

💡 Did You Know?

The human eye can detect motion differences up to roughly 1000 fps in ideal conditions, but the perceptible benefit of going above 240Hz diminishes drastically. Most pro gamers use 240Hz or 360Hz monitors.
