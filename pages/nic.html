🌐 Networking

NIC — Network Interface Card

Your computer's physical gateway to networks — Ethernet at 1G, 2.5G, or 10G.

🎥 Explanatory Media

📖 What is a NIC?

The NIC (or LAN Card) is the physical interface between your computer and the network. It handles data framing, MAC addressing, and signal conversion for Ethernet or Fiber.

💡 Did You Know?

A 10 Gigabit Ethernet (10GbE) NIC can transfer data at 1,250 MB/s — fast enough to completely fill a 1 TB SSD in under 15 minutes over a local network.

📜 History

Evolved from expensive add-in cards in the 1980s to being integrated into every motherboard by the early 2000s.

🏗️ Architecture

Includes a controller chip (ASIC), specialized buffer memory, and physical ports (RJ45, SFP+).
