“– What is a Keyboard?

A keyboard is an input device that lets you type characters, execute commands, and interact with your computer. Modern keyboards come in an enormous variety of form factors, switch types, layouts, and connectivity options — from ultra-portable 60% layouts to full-size numpad keyboards.

’¡ Did You Know?

The QWERTY layout was designed in the 1870s for typewriters — partly to slow typists down to prevent mechanical key jams. Yet it remains the global standard 150 years later.
