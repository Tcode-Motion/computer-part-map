📜 History

Evolved from massive mainframe computers to rack-mounted x86 servers in the 1990s, and now to ultra-dense blade servers and hyper-converged infrastructure in modern data centers.

🏗️ Architecture

Features multi-socket motherboards, ECC (Error-Correcting Code) RAM, redundant power supplies (PSUs), hardware RAID controllers, and baseboard management controllers (BMCs) for out-of-band management.
