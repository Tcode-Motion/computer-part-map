“– What is a Sound Card?

A sound card is a hardware component that processes audio signals — converting digital data into analog sound for speakers/headphones (DAC), and analog input from microphones into digital data (ADC). Modern CPUs include basic integrated audio (Realtek ALC codecs), but dedicated sound cards or external DACs offer dramatically better audio quality, lower noise floor, and more connectivity.
