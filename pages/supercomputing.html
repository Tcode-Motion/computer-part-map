Supercomputer Architectures

Advanced documentation and architecture breakdown for Types of Computers.

📖 Overview

Computers are not just PCs. They range from tiny microcontrollers in appliances to massive supercomputers that occupy entire buildings and perform trillions of calculations per second.

⚙️ Architecture & Working Principle

Varies greatly: Supercomputers use massively parallel clusters; Mainframes use high-reliability redundant logic; Microcontrollers use ultra-low-power integrated designs.

All use basic logic gates but differ in throughput, parallelization, and fault tolerance requirements.
