Printer

From documents to photos — inkjet, laser, and tank printers compared.

📖 What is a Printer?

A printer is an output device that creates physical copies of digital documents and images. Modern printers support wireless printing via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, automatic duplex (double-sided) printing, and multi-function capabilities including scanning, copying, and faxing.

💡 Did You Know?

Printer ink is one of the most expensive liquids in the world — certified OEM inkjet ink can cost over ₹15,000 per 100ml. This is why ink tank printers like the Epson EcoTank series (using cheap refillable ink bottles) have become so popular in India.
