📖 What is a Projector?

A projector is an output device that casts a large image from a computer or media source onto a screen or wall. Used in classrooms, offices, home theaters, and events, modern projectors support Full HD, 4K resolution, and can project images 100–150 inches in size.

💡 Did You Know?

A laser projector bulb never needs replacing and lasts 20,000+ hours (vs 2,000–5,000 hours for lamp projectors). Over 10 years of use, a laser projector saves ₹15,000–₹40,000 in lamp replacement costs.
