📖 What is a PSU?

The Power Supply Unit (PSU) converts AC power from your wall outlet into the DC voltages (3.3V, 5V, 12V) that your computer components require. A quality PSU provides stable, clean power, which directly impacts system stability and component longevity.

💡 Did You Know?

A low-quality PSU is one of the most common causes of random crashes, data corruption, and even component damage. Spending ₹3,000 more on a good PSU can save you ₹30,000 in cooked components.
