🌐 Networking

Router — Network Gateway

Your internet's front door — routes data between your devices and the world.

📖 What is a Router?

A router is a networking device that forwards data packets between computer networks. In a home context, it connects your local network (your phones, laptops, smart TVs) to the internet via your ISP. Modern routers also include built-in Wi-Fi, firewall, DHCP server, and often parental controls and VPN support.

💡 Did You Know?

Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be), releasing in 2024–2025, can theoretically hit speeds of 46 Gbps — fast enough to transfer a full 4K movie in under a second on a local network.
