📖 What is a Scanner?

A scanner is an input device that captures the visual content of physical documents, photos, or objects and converts them into digital files. Used for digitizing paperwork, archiving photos, creating digital signatures, and OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to make scanned documents searchable and editable.

💡 Did You Know?

Modern smartphone cameras can replace basic scanners for documents, but a dedicated flatbed scanner at 1200 DPI can capture fine art and photos with detail that no phone camera can match — resolving individual ink dots and film grain.
