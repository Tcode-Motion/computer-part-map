📖 What are Computer Speakers?

Computer speakers convert electrical audio signals into sound waves. They range from tiny built-in laptop speakers to high-end studio monitors worth lakhs. For desktop setups, a 2.0 or 2.1 system offers excellent stereo sound, while 5.1 setups deliver cinematic surround sound for movies and games.

💡 Did You Know?

A "2.1" speaker system means 2 satellite speakers + 1 subwoofer. The subwoofer handles frequencies below 200 Hz (bass), which requires a much larger driver to reproduce accurately.
