⌨️ Input Device

Touchpad

Your laptop's built-in pointing device — precision, gestures, and haptic feedback.

📖 What is a Touchpad?

A touchpad (or trackpad) is a flat, touch-sensitive surface built into laptops that replaces a mouse. It detects finger position, pressure, and gestures (pinch-to-zoom, two-finger scroll, three-finger swipe). Modern precision touchpads use Microsoft's Precision Touchpad drivers for superior accuracy and gesture support.

💡 Did You Know?

Apple's Force Touch trackpad uses a linear actuator (Taptic Engine) to simulate click feel, despite the surface not actually moving at all. It's an illusion created by vibration patterns.
