📖 What is a USB Flash Drive?

A USB flash drive (also called a pen drive or thumb drive) is a portable NAND flash memory device with a USB connector. Used for transferring files, booting operating systems, storing portable apps, and as quick backups. The key performance difference is which USB standard it uses.

💡 Did You Know?

The first USB flash drive (USB 1.0) was introduced in 2000 with just 8 MB of storage. Today, a single USB-C flash drive can hold 2 TB — 250,000× more data.
