📖 What is a Wi-Fi Adapter?

A Wi-Fi adapter adds wireless connectivity to a desktop PC or any device without built-in Wi-Fi. They come as USB dongles (plug-and-play, portable) or PCIe cards (faster, more stable, with antenna). Modern Wi-Fi 6 and 6E adapters support OFDMA and MU-MIMO for much lower latency in congested environments.

💡 Did You Know?

Wi-Fi 6E adds the 6 GHz band — a completely new, less congested spectrum. In a crowded apartment building, switching from Wi-Fi 5 to Wi-Fi 6E can cut latency by 75%.
